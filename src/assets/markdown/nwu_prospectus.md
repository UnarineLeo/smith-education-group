How to apply for residence placement at NWU?
Applying for residence placement is a two-step
process:
1. Indicate your interest: During your application for
university admission, please select “Yes” or “No” from
the drop-down menu on the “Additional Information”
screen of the application platform when asked:
“Interested in NWU residence placement?”
2. Apply: After receiving and accepting your
conditional offer, you will have the opportunity to apply
for residence via the StarRez Portal, select your top
three (3) residence choices, and provide a motivation
for your application.
•
Please note that the residence options available will
be based on the campus where you received your
conditional offer.
The Residence Department will assess and approve
your application if you meet the criteria. Once your
application is accepted, you must pay a non-refundable
security deposit within 21 days to confirm your place in
the residence.
•
Important note: Students who have applied for NSFAS,
or are funded by another bursary, and can provide proof
thereof, you do not need to pay the deposit.

Calculate your APS
The Admission Point Score (APS) helps universities assess whether you meet the entry requirements for the programme
you’ve applied to. It’s a simple, standardised way to evaluate your academic performance.
How is your APS calculated?
Your APS is calculated using the marks from your six best subjects in Grade 11 or Grade 12 – excluding Life Orientation.
Each subject is awarded points based on the percentage you achieved:

Applying to university might feel overwhelming, but NWU makes the process easy and straightforward. Here’s your
comforting guide to ensure you’re all set for an exciting new chapter!
Helpful tips:
Stay organized:
Keep track of important
dates like application
deadlines to avoid
missing any steps.

1. Create your application profile
Go to the NWU online application portal and create your profile using your email
address. Ensure all details match your ID document.
2. Check your email account for login details
You’ll receive a temporary password. Use this to log in, and feel free to change your
password for extra security.
3. Get oriented
Log in, read the instructions, and then click ‘Apply Now’ to start your application.
4. Choose your study options
Decide whether you want to study full-time or part-time, on-campus or via distance learning. Select up to two study
choices and your preferred campus
5. Fill in personal details
Ensure all personal details match your ID, including your full name, and grant NWU permission to use your info as per
the POPI Act.
6. Provide academic information
Add your most recent high school results. If you’re in Grade 12, use your Grade 11 results.
7. Residence & funding
Indicate how you plan to fund your studies and whether you’d like to apply for residence (hostel placement).
8. Upload documents
Upload scanned copies of required documents (ID, results). Double-check everything before moving on.
9. Submit your application
When you’re sure everything is correct, click “Submit”. Keep your application number safe for future reference.
10. Wait and track your application
After submission, regularly check your email and application portal for updates. If you’re successful, you’ll receive a
conditional offer.
