OUR STORY

Personal guidance.
Lasting confidence.

Smith Education Group began as Smith’s Tutoring, with a simple belief: learning becomes possible when students have the right support. Founded by Kwanda Camane, a Statistics and Data Science graduate from the University of Pretoria, our work is rooted in clear explanations, patient guidance and personal attention.

Today, we support South African learners from primary school to university. Through personalised tutoring, focused revision and academic mentorship, we help students build understanding, strengthen their skills and approach their studies with confidence.

01 / OUR MISSION

Support every next step.

Make quality academic support accessible, engaging and responsive to the way each student learns.

02 / OUR VISION

Open up possibilities.

Build an education community that connects classroom learning with personal growth and future opportunities.

03 / OUR APPROACH

Understand. Practise. Grow.

Start with the learner. Explain the concepts clearly, practise with purpose and build the confidence to work independently.
