OUR STORY

Personal guidance.
Lasting confidence.

Founded in 2022 by Kwanda Camane, a Statistics and Data Science graduate from the University of Pretoria, Smith Education Group began with a simple goal: to help students understand challenging concepts and believe in their abilities.

Previously known as Smith’s Tutoring, we support South African learners from primary school to university through personalised tutoring, revision and academic mentorship.

01 / OUR MISSION

Make learning accessible.

Combine academic expertise, technology and personal support to make quality education more engaging and effective.

02 / OUR VISION

Open up possibilities.

Connect academic learning with personal development and future career opportunities.

03 / OUR APPROACH

Understand. Practise. Grow.

Meet each learner where they are, build understanding and develop confidence that lasts beyond the classroom.
